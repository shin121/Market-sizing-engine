# Mission and scope

The engine answers evidence-backed South Korean audience and market-size questions across five non-interchangeable units: person, child person, household, establishment, and enterprise. It maintains two layers: official/calibrated baseline cells that reconcile to a declared universe, and overlapping commercial archetypes that must never be summed as though mutually exclusive.

An answer is publishable only when it includes Low/Base/High, unit, denominator, period, formula, source releases, assumptions, confidence, and gaps. Missing evidence is `not_estimable`, never zero. Synthetic NVIDIA personas and synthetic minor/household links are sampling and design aids—not facts about actual Koreans.

Version `kr-v0.1.0` proves the full path for an enterprise question: Korean restaurant/bar small businesses, owner age 60–69, and no own website. The exact age and website joint is unavailable, so the result is deliberately D-grade. Other archetypes remain `not_estimable` until their required joints are acquired.

Out of scope are individual identification, eligibility or adverse decisions, behavioral targeting of minors, covert sensitive-attribute inference, and implicit person↔household↔business conversion.
