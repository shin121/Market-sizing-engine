# Confidence and uncertainty

Confidence is a scored explanation, not cosmetic labeling. The PostgreSQL schema allocates points to source quality (30), recency (15), directness (20), joint observation (20), and model reliance (15), totaling 100. Grades are A ≥85, B ≥75, C ≥60, D ≥40, E below 40.

Direct official controls may be A/B after universe review. The vertical slice scores 48/D: its I56 baseline and 60+ owner share are official, but exact 60–69 within the industry and own-website status are not jointly observed. Its Low/Base/High factors therefore include scenario uncertainty and the count is rounded to 10,000.

An E result with NULL counts means “not currently estimable,” not a market of zero. Required gap types include missing joint distribution, weak proxy, unobserved website presence, unobserved owner attribute, unknown unit conversion, purchase intent, spend, geography, small sample, and overlap. Scenario revenue inputs never improve population confidence.
